# Household vocabulary

These are stable names and field shapes, not an exhaustive list of household values. Category names, account names, payer choices, and enabled options continue to come from the relevant consumer or live sheet. The strict `assertShape` projection check rejects undeclared fields so a consumer cannot silently borrow another consumer's subset; it does not replace each source validator's date, business, or option checks.

The common journal names in both ledgers are `txn_id`, `日期`, `金額`, `分類`, `交易對象`, `來源`, and `沖銷txn_id`. The Partner ordered `ENTRY_HEADERS` projection is `txn_id`, `日期`, `金額`, `付款人`, `分攤方式`, `分類`, `交易對象`, `記帳人`, `來源`, `沖銷txn_id`. The Partner `Transaction` payload has required `date` (string), `amount` (number), `payer` (string), `split` (one of `這筆平分`, `幫狗狗付`, `幫自己付`), and `category` (string); `payee` is optional string. Its marker `結清` denotes settlement. Source: partner-ledger `apps-script/Code.gs` `ENTRY_HEADERS` and `functions/lib/validate.ts` `Transaction`, pinned at `e05760808519111abf067cc8fe60f221f6b4df60`.

The Solo ordered `JOURNAL_HEADERS` projection is `日期`, `時間`, `類型`, `借方帳戶`, `貸方帳戶`, `金額`, `幣別`, `分類`, `交易對象`, `說明`, `結清狀態`, `沖銷txn_id`, `txn_id`, `來源`, `建立時間`. `借方帳戶` and `貸方帳戶` are journal legs; `結清狀態` is derived. Its `Transaction` payload requires `type` (`支出`, `收入`, `轉帳`), `amount` (number), `date` (string), and `description` (string). Optional strings are `time`, `account`, `toAccount`, `category`, `payee`, and `currency`; optional `iou` is `應收` or `應付`. Source: solo-ledger `apps-script/Code.gs` `JOURNAL_HEADERS` and `functions/lib/validate.ts` `Transaction`, pinned at `714ee30c61cb65b7f91cc35c444aca7ff5a72e0a`.

The web importer has two ordered Moneybook CSV projections from `web/src/import/read.ts`. `DETAIL_HEADER` / `DetailRow` uses string columns `金融機構/手動新增`, `身分證字號`, `機構名稱`, `帳戶名稱`, `分類`, `明細描述`, `幣別`, `金額`, `消費日`, `入帳日`, `標籤`, `備註`, `帳戶停用時間`. `ACCOUNT_HEADER` / `AccountRow` uses string columns `機構名稱`, `帳戶名稱`, `幣別`, `信用額度`, `帳戶金額`, `可用額度`, `帳戶停用時間`. The source parser retains extra trailing CSV columns; the strict contract projection applies only when checking a known row shape and is not a replacement parser.

The active consumers are `web` (expense-dash), `partner-ledger`, and `solo-ledger`. The separate legacy expense-pwa frontend is deprecated and has no active vocabulary projection or generated artifacts. Its historical source remains recorded in `src/provenance.json`: expense-pwa `functions/lib/validate.ts` and its `create` fixture, pinned at `51b55b77f773e13fb0a8dd5e7896bf56f7502fab`. The legacy signed fixture remains in `vectors/envelopes.json` to check envelope compatibility.
